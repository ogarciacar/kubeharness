# Replay

`docs/figure/kubeharness-replay.html` replays real e2e runs from the traces in `docs/figure/traces/`, one per test and profile: `TestG01Routing`, `TestG02ReferenceGrant` and `TestG03WeightFlip` under Envoy Gateway and agentgateway, and `TestA01Tools` and `TestK01Routing` under agentgateway. The plate carries the run's real Kind cluster name. The top-left corner reads `Replay` and the bottom-left corner the running trace header's `test`, for example `TestG02ReferenceGrant/gateway-api` or `TestA01Tools`, in the trace's casing; it changes with every profile or scenario. The bottom-right corner (`[data-readout]`) sits at the card's right edge, under the name panel, so the longest test name (`TestG02ReferenceGrant/gateway-agentgateway`) never runs into it. It reads two rows, `PINNED` with the profile's pins and `CLUSTER` with `kind v0.33.0 · k8s v1.37.0`. The clock moved to `.kh-stage` `data-clock`, which ends `t+NN.NNs` of real elapsed time and the current rate: `fast ×10` in setup, `slowed ×20`, `slowed ×8` or `×1` in the body, `×1` in cleanup. The top-right corner (`[data-elapsed]`) shows the replay's own elapsed time as `mm:ss.mmm`, video time rather than trace time: it starts at `00:00.000` on each profile or scenario click and ends near `runSeconds()`, so a long install with nothing moving still shows time passing. The title carries a badge (`[data-verdict]`) that reads `RUNNING`, then `PASS` (green) or `FAIL` (red) from the report's `cause`. Lamps, the cartridge, the post lamps, the lever, the gate valve, the MCP port, the model lamps, the display and every request pulse move at their recorded times, on a time map `replay.mjs` derives from the trace. Setup, until the test's `Gateway` call returns, plays 10 times faster: each step holds `gap × 0.1`, and a step that lights a new lamp holds at least 0.6 s, so every lifecycle phase stays visible. Cleanup, from the `Cleanup` call, plays at real time. The test body between them plays at the scenario's rate from `model.mjs` `REPLAY.<scenario>.body`: 20 times slower for G01, G02 and A01, 8 times slower for K01, whose body is about 3 s of chats, and at real time for G03, whose body is about 10 s of traffic. Each body step holds at least 1.2 s. A backend post glows dim once its Pod is Ready. At the end `[data-report]` reads the test's report line verbatim. Only scenarios with a trace have an enabled button. `tour` is disabled.

The card is wider than the scripted one. The left panel is the figure as on the scripted page. The right panel, headed `K8S OBJECTS`, lists the trace's real object names in one group per bench part the scenario uses, each under a numbered pin on that part, headed by its primary object in Kubernetes terms:

| Scenario | Headings |
| --- | --- |
| G01 | `Gateway default/edge`, `HTTPRoute default/shop`, `Service default/orders`, `Service default/payments` |
| G02 | `Gateway shop/edge`, `HTTPRoute shop/pay`, `ReferenceGrant payments/payments`, `Service shop/orders`, `Service payments/payments`, `Namespace payments` |
| G03 | `Gateway shop/edge`, `HTTPRoute shop/checkout`, `Service shop/blue`, `Service shop/green` |
| A01 | `Gateway default/edge`, `HTTPRoute default/mcp`, `AgentgatewayBackend default/calculator` |
| K01 | `Gateway default/edge`, `HTTPRoute default/llm`, `Service default/kronk` (rows: the AgentgatewayBackends `qwen`, `smol` and `gemma`, and kronk's Pod) |

## Sub-features

- `timing` plays setup 10 times faster, cleanup at real time and the body at the scenario's rate. With the defaults `kubeharnessFigure.runSeconds()` is, for the current traces: the table below. Setup is 6.7 to 9.5 s of each, and about 5 s before the `test` lamp, with every lifecycle lamp from `new` to `ready` lit at least 0.6 s. G03 draws 43 (Envoy Gateway) and 42 (agentgateway) of its 204 pulses. K01's longest body step, a 0.56 s chat, holds 4.5 s.

  | Run | `runSeconds()` | Setup | Body | Cleanup | With `?setup=1` |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | G01 Envoy Gateway | 20.7 s | 8.2 s | 10.8 s | 1.7 s | 65.0 s |
  | G01 agentgateway | 19.2 s | 6.9 s | 10.8 s | 1.6 s | 51.2 s |
  | G02 Envoy Gateway | 51.9 s | 8.3 s | 41.9 s | 1.7 s | 96.4 s |
  | G02 agentgateway | 48.8 s | 6.7 s | 40.6 s | 1.6 s | 79.2 s |
  | G03 Envoy Gateway | 38.1 s | 8.2 s | 28.1 s | 1.7 s | 82.4 s |
  | G03 agentgateway | 38.5 s | 8.8 s | 28.1 s | 1.6 s | 87.4 s |
  | A01 agentgateway | 19.3 s | 6.7 s | 10.8 s | 1.7 s | 50.1 s |
  | K01 agentgateway | 46.6 s | 9.5 s | 35.0 s | 2.1 s | 101.9 s |

  Read a run's length with `kubeharnessFigure.runSeconds()` after selecting it. Measure the table again after a trace is re-recorded.
- `plate` engraves the trace header's `cluster`.
- `corners` show `Replay` top left and the trace header's `test` bottom left, updated in `begin()` from `DATA.replay[profile][scenario].test`.
- `pods` puts real Pod names in the say line (`Pod envoy-gateway-system/envoy-gateway-… Ready`) and sets `data-ready` on `[data-post]` when a backend's Pod is Ready.
- `pulses` sends one pulse per recorded request at its recorded offset. A 2xx goes to the post its body names, or, when the body names no backend, the post its path rule names. In K01 no body names a backend, so each 2xx goes to the kronk post by its `/m/<alias>/` path rule. A non-2xx on the gated path stops at the valve. Anything else stops at the diverter. Under G03's cap of 4 pulses a second a step skips pulses that would come sooner than 0.25 s after the last one drawn; the say line keeps the full count.
- `routing` (G01) turns the lever to each request's rule: orders and orders/42 light post a, payments and `payments.example/refunds` light post b, and the unrouted and unknown-host requests flash the diverter.
- `gate` (G02) closes, opens and closes with `ResolvedRefs=False`, `True`, `False`. Without a ReferenceGrant (G01, G03) the valve stays open. A01 and K01 have one backend, so their valve is hidden.
- `proxy-lag` (G02, when the trace holds one) shows a 200 burst that reached `payments` after the route said `ResolvedRefs=False`. The earlier agentgateway recording had one; the current traces do not, and the journey adds that leg only when the trace's steps carry it. The say line reads `… → 200 from payments ×5 · proxy still routing after ResolvedRefs=False`, and the valve does not flash.
- `flip` (G03) moves the lever blue, green, blue. Each `h.Apply(testdata/g03-route/…)` says the weights it sets, and the lever moves when the next `AwaitRoute shop/checkout` reports done. Every stream pulse lights the post whose backend answered.
- `mcp` (A01) lights the MCP port on each request to `/mcp` until `AwaitProbe` returns. The say line names what each response answers (`initialize`, `tools/list = [sum]`, `tools/call = 42`), and the display reads `42` from the `tools/call` body head.
- `models` (K01) lights one of the three model lamps on the kronk post (`[data-model][data-on]`) for each chat answer and writes that model's tag in the display: `QWEN3`, `SMOL2` or `GEMMA`, from `model.mjs` `REPLAY.K01.models`. The lamp is the response's `model` field, which is the id that the routed AgentgatewayBackend pins, and the say line ends `from <model id>`. `/m/unknown/` stops at the diverter with no lamp lit. Under the crossed route `/m/smol/` lights the Qwen lamp. The lamp and tag stay until the test's next call that sends no request, so the bench is dark during `h.Apply(testdata/k01-route/good)`, `gw.AwaitRoute(llm)` and cleanup. The trace does not record kronk's `chat_requests_total` counter, the test's second witness, so the replay does not show it.
- `report` shows the verbatim report line at `off`.
- `heads` heads each group with its primary object, from `groupsOf` and the scenario's `kinds`. The heading shows the kind alone, muted, until the object exists, then its name, and takes the row states below. The primary object is not also a row. No bench-part word (cartridge, diverter, gate valve, post, tray) appears in the panel or its caption.
- `api` shows each object's API version beside its name, smaller, from the `api` its trace lines carry: `gateway.networking.k8s.io/v1` for a Gateway or HTTPRoute, `gateway.networking.k8s.io/v1beta1` for the ReferenceGrant, `agentgateway.dev/v1alpha1` for the AgentgatewayBackend, `v1` for Namespaces, Services and Pods. A version that does not fit beside the name drops whole onto the next line, as the ReferenceGrant's does. A heading's version stays hidden with its name until the object exists. The install count, and a Service only a port-forward names (the proxy's), show none.
- `checks` under the lists has one row per `Await*` call (`.kh-checks[data-on] [data-check]`, label `<fn> <arg>` with the URL host dropped). Its `data-s` is `wait` until the call returns, then `pass` with a green box or `fail` with a red box, from the return's `failed`.
- `pins` in the bottom-right corner reads `PINNED` with the running profile's `model.mjs` `PROFILES.<profile>.pins` (`Gateway API v1.6.1 · Envoy Gateway v1.9.3` or `Gateway API v1.6.1 · agentgateway v1.4.1`), rewritten in `begin()` on every profile change, and `CLUSTER` with `model.mjs` `CLUSTER` (`kind v0.33.0 · k8s v1.37.0`, from `go.mod` and ADR 0030).
- `names` lists each other object from `objectsOf` under its part's pin number, with kind and full name. A row is hidden until the object is applied or Ready, muted while it exists, bright (`data-touched`) while a step touches it, and struck through with `gone` once deleted. The Gateway group ends with the install count, for example `+40 install objects` for Envoy Gateway and `+24 install objects` for agentgateway. The card keeps one height for the whole run.
- `bench pins` shows and numbers only the run's own pins (`[data-pin]` without `data-off`), and lights the pin and the part outline (`[data-pin][data-on]`, `[data-named][data-touched]`) while any of the part's objects is touched. The tray shows only in G02.
- `one backend` (A01, K01) draws the bench with one backend: `BACKENDS.A01.b` and `BACKENDS.K01.b` are `null` in `model.mjs`, so `.kh-bench` carries `data-one-backend` and the front post, line `b`, the diverter's `b` outlet, the gate valve and the tray are hidden, with no `b`, `gate` or `tray` pin. Any scenario whose `BACKENDS` entry has `b: null` gets the same bench.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Build it with `(cd docs/figure && node build.mjs --traces traces && node build.mjs --traces traces --light)`, then open `kubeharness-replay.html`.
- Click `Envoy Gateway` or `agentgateway`, then a scenario. `A01` and `K01` are enabled only under agentgateway.
- Add `?setup=N` to play setup `N` times faster (default 10, `1` for real time with no phase hold), `?body=N` to play the body `N` times slower (overriding the scenario's rate), `?hold=S` to hold each body step at least `S` seconds (default 1.2), and `?speed=S` to divide every hold by `S` (default 1).

## Driving it with drive.sh

Preconditions:

- Doctor exits 0. Its `replay …` lines are ready, which means `docs/figure/traces/*.jsonl` exist and the replay pages were just built.
- `journeys/replay.json` matches the committed traces. If a trace was re-recorded, run `node .cursor/skills/verify-kubeharness-figure/scripts/replay-journey.mjs` first.

- **Run it.** `scripts/drive.sh replay --replay journeys/replay.json`. It plays every recorded run once, profile by profile, G01, G02, G03, then A01 and K01 under agentgateway, with the default time maps. It takes about five minutes.
- **Every leg.** It clicks the profile and scenario buttons, then waits `until` the bottom-left corner's `innerText` equals the trace header's `test` and the top-left corner's is `Replay`, that the panel heading reads `K8S OBJECTS`, and that the test name ends left of the readout. Then it evals the corners, the buttons (`… A01:disabled K01:disabled tour:disabled` under Envoy Gateway), the plate (the trace's cluster), the headings with kinds alone, that the panel has no bench-part word, the shown pins, that the bottom-right corner reads `PINNED`, the profile's pins, `CLUSTER` and `kind v0.33.0 · k8s v1.37.0`, that the check labels are the trace's `Await*` calls, that every box is `wait` and the badge `RUNNING`, and that the elapsed corner reads `mm:ss.mmm` under 2 s. Once the `test` lamp is lit (`replay-<p>-<scenario>-install.png`), an `until` waits (up to 60 s; the Pods are Ready 6 to 16 s of trace time after `New` returns, under 2 s at the fast setup rate) for each post's Pod row to be `present` with the trace's Pod name, then for the HTTPRoute heading's `.kh-api` to be visible with the trace's api, `gateway.networking.k8s.io/v1`. Each leg ends on an `until` that the report line equals the trace's `report` line, an `until` that each box's `data-s` and painted colour (`green`, `red` or none, matched against `--anatomy-green` and `--anatomy-red`) equal what the trace's returns say, an `until` that the badge reads the trace's verdict in its colour, an `until` that the lamp row ends all `done`, or with the stopped lamp `fail` for a failing trace, an `until` that the elapsed corner is within 3 s under `runSeconds()`, a shot (`replay-<p>-<scenario>-off.png`), then an `until` that the card is 625 px high, as it also is at the `test` lamp. `scripts/replay-journey.mjs` `CARD_HEIGHT_PX` holds that number for every run, so a name list or a check list that outgrows the bench fails its leg.
- **G01.** The headings read their names. Then `until` the say line reads `GET http://shop.example/orders/42 → 200 from orders` with post a lit (`replay-<p>-G01-orders.png`), `GET http://payments.example/refunds → 200 from payments` with post b lit (`…-G01-payments.png`), and the unknown-host request with the diverter flashing (`…-G01-unrouted.png`).
- **G02.** The gate eval reads `closed → open → closed`. `replay-<p>-G02-denied.png` shows pulses stopping at the closed valve. The ReferenceGrant heading becomes `present` and `data-touched` with the gate pin and the valve outline lit and its `.kh-api` visible as `gateway.networking.k8s.io/v1beta1` (`…-G02-grant-row.png`), and the stem transform reaches a y below -0.5. When the trace has a proxy-lag burst, `…-G02-proxy-lag.png` shows pulses reaching the front post while the valve is closed. After the revoke, the heading is `gone` with its `gone` tag visible.
- **G03.** The lever eval reads `a → b → a`. Then `until` `state().lever` is `a` with post a lit (`replay-<p>-G03-blue.png`), `b` with post b lit (`…-G03-green.png`), and `a` again (`…-G03-blue-again.png`).
- **A01.** `until` the MCP port is lit (`replay-agw-A01-mcp.png`), then `until` the display reads `42` and the say line `tools/call = 42` (`replay-agw-A01-42.png`).
- **K01.** `until` `.kh-bench` carries `data-models`. Then, for `qwen`, `smol` and `gemma` in turn, `until` the say line has `POST http://llm.example/m/<alias>/` and `from <model id>`, the display reads the model's tag and the lamp row reads `100`, `010`, then `001` (`replay-agw-K01-qwen.png`, `…-smol.png`, `…-gemma.png`). `until` `/m/unknown/` flashes the diverter with the row `000` (`…-K01-unknown.png`). `until` `/m/smol/` is answered `from unsloth/Qwen3-0.6B-Q8_0` with `QWEN3` and `100` (`…-K01-crossed.png`). `until` the say line has `h.Apply(testdata/k01-route/good)` with a blank display and `000` (`…-K01-reapply.png`), and the same at `gw.AwaitRoute(llm)`. `until` `/m/smol/` is answered by SmolLM2 again with `010` (`…-K01-restored.png`), then `until` the bench is dark at cleanup. `scripts/replay-journey.mjs` reads which request was crossed from the trace and the `route.yaml` applied before it. For a passing K01 trace with no crossed answer it stops with an error that names the test and the cluster.
- **Failing run.** `docs/figure/traces-failing/G02-gateway-api.jsonl` is a real G02 run with a broken grant fixture (see that directory's README). Build both themes with `(cd docs/figure && node build.mjs --traces traces-failing && node build.mjs --traces traces-failing --light)`. Run `scripts/drive.sh replay-failing --replay journeys/replay-failing.json`, and `scripts/drive.sh replay-failing --light --replay journeys/replay-failing.json` for the light page. Then rebuild both themes from `traces` with `(cd docs/figure && node build.mjs --traces traces && node build.mjs --traces traces --light)`, or run doctor. The journey comes from `node scripts/replay-journey.mjs docs/figure/traces-failing journeys/replay-failing.json` and proves the red box and the red `FAIL` badge.
- **Video.** `scripts/record.sh <profile> <scenario> --replay [--speed S] [--setup N] [--body N] [--hold S]`, for example `scripts/record.sh gateway-agentgateway K01 --replay`. Its duration is `runSeconds()`: the sum over setup steps of `gap ÷ N`, at least 0.6 s on a step that lights a new lamp, plus the sum over body steps of `max(gap × rate, hold)`, plus cleanup. Check it with `node docs/figure/tools/phases-check.mjs docs/figure/traces/<scenario>-<profile>.jsonl <scenario> <video>.phases.txt [fps] [N]`. It compares every phase with the time map frame by frame, and `provisioning` and `wait-usable` with the trace's own phase events: real span ÷ `N`, plus whatever the 0.6 s phase hold adds to the phase's first step. It reads `N` from `-setupN` in the file name when the argument is missing, else 10.

## Gotchas

- In `docs/figure/traces` the G01, G02 and A01 bodies are about a second or less of a 38 to 55 s run, and K01's is about 3 s of a 69 s run. That is why they play slowed. With `?body=1&hold=0` they play at real time again and most steps last a few milliseconds.
- The body starts at `Gateway`'s return, not at `New`'s. `InstallGateway`, the image loads, the applies and the Gateway wait come after `New` returns and take 12 to 39 s (K01, which loads the kronk image, is the longest), which at 20 times slower would add 4 to 13 minutes to the video.
- G03 at `?body=20` is over 3 minutes of body. Its default is `×1`.
- Steps that start in the same millisecond merge, and the last event's say line wins. A G03 route wait that reports done in the millisecond `AwaitResponse` starts shows only the request's line, but the lever still moves on that step.
- A lamp change keeps its own step even in the same millisecond: `new` and `validating` share millisecond 0, and under agentgateway `ready`, `New`'s return and `InstallGateway`'s call often share one. Each lamp keeps its own step, held 0.6 s in setup. With `?setup=1` there is no phase hold, so `new`, `validating` and `ready` last under one frame, and a lamp change in the body or cleanup is a zero-length step.
- In setup the steps play 10 times faster, so a row's bright state can last well under a frame there. Look for bright rows in the body.
- `record.sh` films `ceil(runSeconds × fps)` frames, so the last frame can already be the next loop's `validating`.
